What is going on

Each komodo has a tiny brain: a handful of numbers. It looks at five things (its height, its speed, the distance to the next pipe, how far the gap is above or below it, and a constant) and decides to flap or not.

Generation 1 gets random numbers, so most crash at once. The komodos that flew furthest become parents. Children mix their parents' numbers and get a few random nudges. Repeat. Nobody ever writes the rule for when to flap.

This is a genetic algorithm training a neural network. You will meet both properly later in the course.
